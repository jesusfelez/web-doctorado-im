---
title: "I'm a researcher"
description: "Guide for programme researchers: joining, recruiting candidates, supervision, monitoring and thesis defence."
resumen: "What the programme expects from you as a **supervisor or tutor**, in the order you will need it, with the templates and platforms for each procedure."
---

<ol class="ruta">
<li><span class="r-cuando">To supervise in the programme</span>
<h3>Join and keep your profile up to date</h3>
<p>Researchers join and leave the programme through THESIS, with approval from the Academic Committee. Check that your ORCID and research line are correct in the list.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/programa/investigadores/">Researchers</a> · <a href="/en/programa/lineas/">Research lines</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">Before admission</span>
<h3>Sign the applicant's commitment letter</h3>
<p>By agreement of the Academic Committee, every application must include the supervision and tutoring commitment letter: <a href="/documentos/Carta_compromiso_director_tutor_misma_persona_IM.docx">template if you are both supervisor and tutor</a> or <a href="/documentos/Carta_compromiso_director_tutor_distintos_IM.docx">if they are different people</a> (both sign). It states the research line, provisional title and planned funding.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/admision/requisitos/">Requirements</a> · <a href="/en/admision/criterios/">Admission criteria</a> · <a href="/en/doctorandos/ayudas/">Grants and contracts</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">First 6 months</span>
<h3>Sign the supervision agreement</h3>
<p>With the PhD candidate and UPM. Remember: a thesis can have at most two supervisors, and co-supervision must be justified.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/tesis/direccion/">Supervision, co-supervision and tutoring</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">Before the end of year one</span>
<h3>Endorse the initial research plan</h3>
<p>Help prepare the plan with the <a href="/documentos/Plan_investigacion_inicial_IM.docx">programme template</a> and endorse it in THESIS together with the tutor.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/doctorandos/seguimiento/">Monitoring</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">Every year</span>
<h3>Report on the annual plan and review activities</h3>
<p>Issue your report on the annual plan (usually June–July) and sign the <a href="/documentos/Registro_actividades_doctorando_IM.xlsx">activity log</a>. Check that the three compulsory activities are completed on time.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/doctorandos/seguimiento/">Annual assessment</a> · <a href="/en/doctorandos/formacion/">Training activities and recommended courses</a> · <a href="/en/doctorandos/permanencia/">Deadlines and extensions</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">During the thesis</span>
<h3>Stay abroad, international mention and publications</h3>
<p>Help find a host institution for the 3-month stay. Steer publications towards the quality requirement: at least 1 JCR point, with at most two papers.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/programa/internacionalizacion/">Host institutions</a> · <a href="/en/tesis/mencion-internacional/">International mention</a> · <a href="/en/tesis/requisitos/">Quality requirements</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">At the end</span>
<h3>Pre-assessment, committee and defence</h3>
<p>Propose two experts from outside UPM for the pre-assessment and then the examination committee. Everything is processed in THESIS.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/tesis/deposito-defensa/">Deposit and defence</a> · <a href="/en/tesis/normativa/">Regulations</a></p></li>
</ol>
