**2023-24 in detail:**

- **25 places** for new students offered every year.
- **20 theses defended**: 18 with *sobresaliente cum laude* and **9 with the international mention** (45%).
- **Overall PhD candidate satisfaction: 8.35 out of 10** (survey with 20 responses, indicative value).
- **63 lecturers and researchers** in the programme.

Source: [annual monitoring report 2023-24](https://www.industriales.upm.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/InformeSeguimiento_DIMEC_2023-2024_CAPD_WEB.pdf).
