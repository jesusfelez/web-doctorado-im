## Quality functions of the Academic Committee

The [programme's Academic Committee (CAPD)](/en/calidad/comisiones/) is responsible for:

- Checking that theses proposed for defence meet the quality requirements of the verified report.
- Monitoring the programme every year and preparing accreditation processes.
- Analysing indicators: success rates, duration, drop-out, mobility and mentions.
- Monitoring the employment of our doctors.
- Analysing satisfaction surveys of PhD students and faculty.
- Handling complaints, suggestions and claims.
- Preparing the **annual monitoring report** with improvement proposals, which feeds into the ETSII quality system.
