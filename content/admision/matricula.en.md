## Deadlines and application

{{< general fuente="UPM" enlace="https://www.upm.es/Estudiantes/Estudios_Titulaciones/Estudios_Doctorado/informacion" textoenlace="Calendar and admission application" >}}
- There is a **first application period** each academic year and, if places remain, a **second period**.
- Applications and documents are submitted online at UPM.
- Exact dates are published each year on the International Doctoral School website.
{{< /general >}}

## Enrolment

{{< general fuente="UPM" enlace="http://www.upm.es/sfs/Rectorado/Vicerrectorado%20de%20Investigacion/Doctorado/Admision%20y%20Calendario/NORMATIVA%20MATRICULA%20DOCTORADO2.pdf" textoenlace="Enrolment and admission regulations (in Spanish)" >}}
- First enrolment within the period stated in the admission decision.
- **Annual academic supervision enrolment** for as long as you remain in the programme. If you do not pay it, you are withdrawn.
- Public fees are set each year by the Community of Madrid.
{{< /general >}}

## Full time or part time

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 3 · RD 576/2023" enlace="https://www.upm.es/gsfs/SFS12775" textoenlace="Change of study mode procedure (UPM)" >}}
You state your study mode when you apply. Part-time study requires **authorisation from the Academic Committee**. You can request a change of study mode later. See durations in [Duration and continuation](/en/doctorandos/permanencia/).
{{< /general >}}

## After enrolling

{{< clave >}}Within **6 months** of your first enrolment you must sign the **supervision agreement** with your supervisor and your tutor. Before **one year** has passed you must submit your **research plan and personal training plan**. See [Research plan and monitoring](/en/doctorandos/seguimiento/).{{< /clave >}}
