## Preferred entry profiles (direct access)

{{< programa titulo="Programme-specific · no bridging courses" >}}
Science or technology degrees in mechanical engineering or directly linked to the programme's research lines, for example:

- Mechanical engineering; industrial engineering in its various specialities; energy engineering
- Product development engineering; materials engineering; biomedical engineering
- Automotive, vehicle and transport engineering, and aeronautical engineering
- Laser process, manufacturing and acoustic engineering
- Architecture (construction and architectural technology, building and refurbishment specialities)
- Civil engineering and mining engineering
{{< /programa >}}

## Other profiles (with bridging courses)

{{< programa titulo="Programme-specific · with bridging courses" >}}
Degrees partly related to the programme's research lines, for example:

- Computer, chemical, industrial management, electronic and telecommunications engineering
- Agricultural, forestry and environmental engineering, and biotechnology
- Physics, chemistry, biology and mathematics
- Medicine and sports science (because of their biomechanical implications)
{{< /programa >}}

## Bridging courses

{{< programa >}}
- Between **2 and 3 courses (6 to 9 ECTS)** depending on your previous training and experience.
- They are chosen from the following courses of the **UPM Master's Degree in Mechanical Engineering**:
  - Computer-aided mechanical engineering
  - Advanced simulation methods
  - Manufacturing systems design
  - Transport engineering
- Your **tutor** proposes them according to your background and research line, with the approval of the **Academic Committee**.
- You must **pass them in your first year**. This is a requirement for the pre-assessment of the thesis.
{{< /programa >}}

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 7" >}}
For public fees and grants, bridging courses count as **doctoral-level training**.
{{< /general >}}
