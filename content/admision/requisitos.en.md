## General requirements

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 6 (amended by RD 576/2023)" enlace="https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2011-2541" >}}
**General rule:** official Spanish **bachelor's (Grado) and master's (Máster universitario)** degrees, or equivalent, with at least **300 ECTS** between them.

Access is also open to those who:

- hold official Spanish university degrees with **300 ECTS** at MECES level 3;
- hold a degree from a country in the **EHEA** at level 7 of the European Qualifications Framework that gives access to doctoral studies in that country, without needing recognition;
- hold a degree from **outside the EHEA**, without recognition, if the university confirms that it is equivalent to a Spanish master's degree and gives access to doctoral studies in the country of origin;
- already hold **another doctoral degree**;
- have completed at least **two years of specialised health training** (MIR, FIR…) with a positive assessment.

Admission with a foreign degree **does not imply its recognition** for any other purpose.
{{< /general >}}

{{< general fuente="UPM" enlace="https://www.upm.es/Estudiantes/Estudios_Titulaciones/Estudios_Doctorado/informacion" textoenlace="Access, admission and documents at UPM" >}}
The **International Doctoral School** sets the procedure and deadlines and checks whether each application meets the entry requirements.
{{< /general >}}

## Programme-specific requirements

{{< programa >}}
- **English at B2 level** or higher.
- **Endorsement from a programme researcher** as your potential thesis supervisor.
{{< /programa >}}

{{< clave >}}**Tip:** contact your potential supervisor **before** you apply. Without their endorsement, your application cannot be accepted.{{< /clave >}}
