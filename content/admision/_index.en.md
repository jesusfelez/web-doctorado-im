## The process in 5 steps

{{< plataforma p="tramites" >}}Access and admission application{{< /plataforma >}}

1. **Choose a research line and find a supervisor.** Look at the [research lines](/en/programa/lineas/) and contact a [programme researcher](/en/programa/investigadores/) willing to endorse your application as your potential thesis supervisor and sign the supervision and tutoring commitment letter ([same person](/documentos/Carta_compromiso_director_tutor_misma_persona_IM.docx) or [different people](/documentos/Carta_compromiso_director_tutor_distintos_IM.docx)). **The letter is compulsory and must be attached to your application.**
2. **Check that you meet the entry requirements.** Usually a bachelor's and a master's degree adding up to 300 ECTS are enough. See the [requirements](/en/admision/requisitos/).
3. **Apply for admission at UPM** within the EID deadlines. There is a first application period each year and a second one if places remain. See the [UPM calendar](https://www.upm.es/Estudiantes/Estudios_Titulaciones/Estudios_Doctorado/informacion).
4. **The Academic Committee assesses your application** using the [admission criteria](/en/admision/criterios/) and tells you whether you need [bridging courses](/en/admision/perfiles/).
5. **Enrol** within the period stated in the admission decision. Admission is only effective once you enrol. See [application and enrolment](/en/admision/matricula/).

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011 and UPM" >}}
**5 % of places** are reserved for students with a recognised disability of 33 % or more or with permanent special educational needs.
