{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 4" >}}
Doctoral training does not need to be structured in ECTS credits. It includes transferable and specific training, but your main activity is **research**. Everything is logged in your **activity record**.
{{< /general >}}

## Compulsory activities

### 1. Scientific information and documentation
Searching for and assessing scientific information, databases, reference managers, state-of-the-art reviews and dissemination in papers and conferences.

{{< cursos actividad="1" >}}

### 2. R&D&I management
Research planning and funding, agile methodologies, technology watch, knowledge transfer, intellectual and industrial property, data protection and research ethics.

{{< cursos actividad="2" >}}

### 3. Dissemination of results and promotion of open science
FAIR principles, open-access publishing and repositories (Zenodo, GitHub, GrabCAD…), open licences, social impact and citizen science.

{{< cursos actividad="3" >}}

{{< programa titulo="How they are certified" >}}
- Courses from the **International Doctoral School**, UPM schools, departments and libraries are valid, as are **online courses from reputable institutions** with a certificate of achievement or hours.
- Provide the **certificate** and a **short report** on the content and how it relates to your thesis. For activity 3, add the evidence: repository deposits, data management plan, etc.
- Under each activity you will find **recommended courses and seminars**, free and online. They are only suggestions: you may propose equivalent ones. UPM MOOCs give a free diploma; on Coursera and edX the certificate is usually paid.
- Include them in your **annual research plan**. Your supervisor endorses them and the Academic Committee assesses them.
{{< /programa >}}

## Optional activities

### 4. Stay at an institution abroad
At least **480 hours (about 3 months)**, preferably from the **2nd year** onwards and for three consecutive months, although it can be split. Certify it with a certificate from the host institution and a report on the stay. More information in [Mobility](/en/doctorandos/movilidad/).

### 5. Specific training in your research line
Specialised seminars or courses proposed by your supervisor and approved by the **Academic Committee**: up to 1 ECTS per seminar and a maximum of 6 ECTS.
