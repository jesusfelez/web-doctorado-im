## Maximum duration

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 3 (amended by RD 576/2023)" enlace="https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2011-2541" >}}
<div class="tabla-env"><table>
<thead><tr><th>Study mode</th><th>Maximum duration</th><th>With extension</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Full time</td><td><strong>4 years</strong></td><td>5 years</td></tr>
<tr><td>Part time (authorised by the Academic Committee)</td><td><strong>7 years</strong></td><td>8 years</td></tr>
<tr><td>Disability ≥ 33 %, full time</td><td><strong>6 years</strong></td><td>7 years</td></tr>
<tr><td>Disability ≥ 33 %, part time</td><td><strong>9 years</strong></td><td>10 years</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

- The period runs **from the enrolment date to the thesis submission date**.
- **Extension:** if you have not yet requested submission, you can ask the Academic Committee for **one more year**, always **before the deadline expires**.
- **The period is suspended by:** temporary incapacity, birth, adoption, foster care, risk during pregnancy or breastfeeding, gender-based violence and other situations provided for in the regulations.
- **Temporary leave:** you can request temporary leave of **up to two years in total**, justified to the Academic Committee.
{{< /general >}}

{{< clave >}}**Did you start before 19 July 2023?** Royal Decree 576/2023 provides that candidates who had already started their doctorate when it came into force keep the rules in force when they started (for example, 3 years full time and 5 part time, with their extensions). The exception is the rules on the **examination committee, defence and assessment of the thesis**, which apply to everyone from the **2024-25** academic year. If you are unsure about your case, write to the programme office.{{< /clave >}}

## UPM regulations and procedures

{{< plataforma p="thesis" >}}Extension requests, temporary leave and change of study mode{{< /plataforma >}}

{{< general fuente="UPM" enlace="https://www.upm.es/gsfs/SFS12774" textoenlace="Regulations on continuation in doctoral studies (consolidated text, in Spanish)" >}}
UPM's continuation regulations set out how to request extensions, temporary leave and changes of study mode, and the grounds for permanent withdrawal:

- Not paying the academic supervision fee.
- Not submitting the annual plan, or two consecutive negative assessments.
- Not submitting the thesis within the maximum period.
- Disciplinary sanction.
- At your own request.
