{{< programa titulo="Stays in the programme" >}}
- **Duration:** at least 480 h (≈ 3 months). Ideally in a single period, although it can be split.
- **When:** preferably from the **second year** onwards, once the thesis objectives are defined.
- **Where:** universities, research centres or technology institutes abroad related to your thesis.
- **Before:** the stay must be **endorsed by your supervisor** and **authorised by the Academic Committee**.
- **After:** provide a certificate from the institution with the dates and a report on the stay, and include it in your annual plan.
{{< /programa >}}

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 15 (amended by RD 576/2023)" enlace="https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2011-2541" >}}
For the **international mention**, stays must add up to **at least 3 months outside Spain**. If you do several, one must last **at least one month**. The host institution must validate them and they must appear in your activity record. See [International mention and cotutelle](/en/tesis/mencion-internacional/).
{{< /general >}}

## Funding

Your group's research projects, contracts and agreements, UPM mobility programmes, regional, national (for example, stays for FPU and FPI contract holders) and international grants, and programmes of the host institution itself. See [Grants and funding](/en/doctorandos/ayudas/).
