## 1. Supervision agreement

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 11 · UPM" enlace="https://www.upm.es/gsfs/SFS26180" textoenlace="Model training and supervision agreement (UPM)" >}}
It is signed by the university, you, your supervisor and your tutor. It includes a conflict-resolution procedure, intellectual and industrial property rights and confidentiality, and the commitment to comply with UPM doctoral regulations and the EID Code of Good Practice. An English version is available.
{{< /general >}}

{{< programa >}}
Sign it **as soon as possible** and in any case within **6 months** of your first enrolment.
{{< /programa >}}

## 2. Research plan and personal training plan

{{< plataforma p="thesis" >}}Annual research plan{{< /plataforma >}}

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 11 (amended by RD 576/2023)" >}}
**Before your first year** from enrolment is up, and with help from your supervisor and tutor, you will submit a document containing:

- **Research plan:** methodology, objectives, resources and timeline.
- **Personal training plan:** planned courses, seminars, mobility and other activities.

You can improve it during the doctorate. It must be endorsed by your supervisor and tutor. If you are aiming for the industrial doctorate mention, its specific regulations also apply.
{{< /general >}}

{{< programa titulo="Programme template" >}}
Use the **[initial research plan template](/documentos/Plan_investigacion_inicial_IM.docx)** (Word, bilingual). It includes the sections the Academic Committee asks for and your personal training plan by activity.
{{< /programa >}}

## 3. Activity record

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 2.7" >}}
From the start of your thesis you will log all your training activities and anything relevant to the thesis. Your supervisor and tutor review it regularly. The examination committee assesses it qualitatively, not with a mark.
{{< /general >}}

{{< programa titulo="Activity log in Excel" >}}
Keep your activities in the **[activity log](/documentos/Registro_actividades_doctorando_IM.xlsx)**. It adds up the hours and ECTS of each activity and shows which are still pending. Submit it with each annual plan, signed by your supervisor and with the certificates. (The spreadsheet is in Spanish.)
{{< /programa >}}

## 4. Annual assessment

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 11" enlace="https://www.upm.es/Estudiantes/Estudios_Titulaciones/Estudios_Doctorado/PlanesAnuales" textoenlace="Annual research plans (UPM)" >}}
- Every year the Academic Committee assesses your research plan and activity record, together with reports from your supervisor and tutor.
- If the assessment is **negative**, you are reassessed within a maximum of **6 months**.
- A **second negative assessment** means **permanent withdrawal** from the programme, with a reasoned report and a prior hearing.
{{< /general >}}

{{< programa >}}
- You will present your results for the year at the programme's **annual monitoring sessions**.
- UPM sets the submission deadline each year, usually in **June and July**. **Failing to submit the plan on time means withdrawal.**
{{< /programa >}}
