{{< plataforma p="thesis" >}}Industrial Doctorate mention request{{< /plataforma >}}

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 15 bis · UPM regulations" enlace="https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2011-2541" >}}
The degree may carry the **"Industrial Doctorate"** mention if **all** of the following conditions are met:

1. The thesis develops a **project of industrial, commercial, social or cultural interest** to a company, organisation or public body that benefits directly from it, in addition to UPM.
2. You have **authorisation from the EID Management Committee** to do an industrial doctorate. Its industrial nature must be clear in the first annual plan after authorisation.
3. An industrial doctorate **agreement** is signed within **two years** of your first enrolment.
4. You have a **UPM supervisor** and a **person in charge appointed by the company**, who may co-supervise the thesis if eligible. That person **cannot sit on the examination committee**, although someone else from the company may.
5. You have held a **contract** with the company or organisation for **at least one year** during the thesis, and a substantial part of the thesis was carried out there.
{{< /general >}}

{{< general fuente="UPM" enlace="https://www.upm.es/sfs/Rectorado/Vicerrectorado%20de%20Investigacion/Doctorado/Normativa/Documentos/MODELO_CONVENIO_colaboracion.pdf" textoenlace="Model collaboration agreement (UPM, in Spanish)" >}}
If the company requires it, the thesis may follow the **confidentiality procedure**. In that case two versions are submitted: a complete confidential one and a public one.
{{< /general >}}

{{< general fuente="Funding" enlace="https://www.aei.gob.es/" textoenlace="State Research Agency – Industrial Doctorates" >}}
There are specific funding calls for industrial doctorates, such as those of the State Research Agency (AEI) and the Community of Madrid.
