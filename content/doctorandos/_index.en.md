## Your doctorate timeline

<div class="tabla-env"><table>
<thead><tr><th>When</th><th>What you need to do</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>On enrolment</td><td>Open your <strong>activity record</strong>, where you will log all your training.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Within the first 6 months</td><td>Sign the <strong>supervision agreement</strong> with your supervisor and your tutor.</td></tr>
<tr><td>First year</td><td>Pass any <strong>bridging courses</strong> assigned to you and submit your <strong>research plan and personal training plan</strong>.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Every year</td><td><strong>Annual assessment</strong> by the Academic Committee: research plan, activity record and reports from your supervisor and tutor.</td></tr>
<tr><td>From year 2</td><td>International stay: recommended, and compulsory if you want the international mention.</td></tr>
<tr><td>After 12 months</td><td>You can request the <strong>pre-assessment</strong> of your thesis, provided all your annual reports have been assessed positively.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Within 4 years (FT) or 7 years (PT)</td><td><strong>Thesis submission</strong>, with a possible one-year extension.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
