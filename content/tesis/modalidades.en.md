{{< programa titulo="Programme report (2026), section 5.3" >}}
The programme allows **both thesis formats, by monograph and by compendium of scientific publications**, in accordance with UPM regulations.
{{< /programa >}}

## Thesis by monograph

This is the usual format. The thesis is submitted as **a single unified document** presenting the complete research work. It is usually organised into introduction and state of the art, objectives and hypotheses, methodology, results and discussion, conclusions and future work, and references.

{{< general fuente="UPM · Regulations on the preparation and assessment of the doctoral thesis" enlace="https://www.upm.es/gsfs/SFS57181" >}}
- **Language:** preferably Spanish. It may also be written and defended in English, or in another language with justification. In that case a summary in Spanish and English is added.
- **Format:** submitted as a **PDF**. Any supplementary material goes in a ZIP file.
- **Summary:** in Spanish and English, **up to 4,000 characters**.
{{< /general >}}

## Thesis by compendium of publications

The thesis is made up of **papers published or accepted** during the doctorate that form a clear thematic unit, together with texts that present and integrate them.

{{< general fuente="UPM · Doctoral thesis by compendium of publications (Governing Council, 30/11/2017)" enlace="https://www.upm.es/gsfs/SFS57196" >}}
**Publication requirements:**

- **At least three papers** on which you are **first author**, in **Q1 or Q2** journals in JCR or Scopus. The more favourable position at submission or at deposit is used.
- Published during the thesis period, with a clear **thematic unit**.
- **No more than six authors** per paper.
- Your **supervisor or co-supervisor** must be an author of **at least one** of the papers.
- **UPM affiliation** on the papers, which may be shared with another institution.
- At deposit, **at least one paper must be published**. The others (no more than two) may be accepted in their final version with a **DOI** assigned.

**Structure of the document:**

1. Summary in Spanish and English.
2. General introduction: state of knowledge, interest of the research, objectives and hypotheses.
3. Methodology.
4. Copies of the publications, in a coherent order. Any paper not in Spanish or English must include a version in one of those languages.
5. General discussion: integrated solution, analysis of results, conclusions, future work and the contribution of each paper.
6. References.
7. Annexes (optional).

The introduction, methodology and general discussion must total **more than 18,000 words** and refer to the papers to show their thematic unity.

**Copyright:** including the accepted manuscript is recommended, stating journal, DOI, volume, year and pages.

The defence follows the same deadlines, examination committee and grades as a thesis by monograph.
