## Thesis formats

You can present the thesis **as a monograph** or **as a compendium of publications**. The requirements for each are in [Thesis formats](/en/tesis/modalidades/).

## When can I request the pre-assessment?

{{< plataforma p="thesis" >}}Pre-assessment request{{< /plataforma >}}

{{< programa >}}
When **at least 12 months** have passed since your first enrolment, you have submitted **all your annual plans** and **all have been assessed positively**.
{{< /programa >}}

## What the Academic Committee checks

{{< programa titulo="Programme checklist" >}}
- [ ] **Bridging courses** passed, if any were assigned.
- [ ] **Scientific output** meeting UPM and programme quality criteria (see below).
- [ ] **Full CV** on the [UPM Scientific Portal](https://portalcientifico.upm.es/).
- [ ] **Training activities** completed and activity record up to date.
- [ ] The thesis follows the **UPM thesis format**.
- [ ] Where applicable, the requirements for the **international mention, cotutelle or industrial doctorate**.
- [ ] **Justified proposal of two external reviewers from outside UPM**, doctors who are experts in the field.
{{< /programa >}}

## Programme publication criterion

{{< programa titulo="Programme-specific · minimum 1 point, with a maximum of 2 papers" >}}
You must have published your results in **journals of recognised quality or indexed** in leading databases (for example, JCR), or on results-publishing platforms such as **Open Research Europe**.

<div class="tabla-env"><table>
<thead><tr><th>Merit</th><th>Points</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Journal in the first quartile (Q1) of its category</td><td><strong>1</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td>Journal in Q2</td><td><strong>0.75</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td>Journal in Q3</td><td><strong>0.5</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td>Journal in Q4</td><td><strong>0.25</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td>International patent, granted after substantive examination and being exploited</td><td>Equivalent to a Q1</td></tr>
<tr><td>National patent granted after substantive examination</td><td>Equivalent to a Q2</td></tr>
<tr><td>Patent granted without substantive examination</td><td>Does not count</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

**Examples:** one Q1 paper meets the requirement; two Q2 papers add up to 1.5 and also meet it; one Q2 and one Q4 add up to 1 and meet it; two Q3 papers add up to 1 and meet it.
{{< /programa >}}

## External reviewers

{{< programa >}}
- Two doctors who are **experts in the field and external to UPM**.
- They **cannot** have been your co-authors during the thesis, be subject to grounds for abstention (art. 23 of Law 40/2015) or be retired researchers with no affiliation to an institution or company.
- They may later sit on the examination committee.
{{< /programa >}}

{{< general fuente="UPM" enlace="https://www.upm.es/gsfs/SFS57179" textoenlace="Quality criteria for doctoral theses (UPM)" >}}
In addition to the programme requirements, the thesis must meet the **general UPM quality criteria**, as amended by the Governing Council on 26/01/2023.
