## Tutor and supervisor

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 11 and 12" enlace="https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2011-2541" >}}
- **Tutor:** a doctor with proven research experience who looks after your relationship with the Academic Committee.
- **Supervisor:** the person ultimately responsible for your research training and for the coherence of the thesis.
- The Academic Committee may change the tutor or supervisor at any time for justified reasons, after hearing the PhD student.
{{< /general >}}

{{< programa >}}
- The supervisor must be a **programme researcher**, Spanish or foreign, with proven research experience. The tutor must also belong to the programme.
- For **theses with the industrial mention**: a UPM supervisor and a person in charge from the company or organisation, who may co-supervise but can **never sit on the examination committee**.
{{< /programa >}}

## Co-supervision

{{< clave >}}**A thesis may have at most two supervisors.** Co-supervision is **not automatic**: it must be requested, justified and authorised.{{< /clave >}}

{{< general fuente="UPM · Co-supervision request procedure (Governing Council, 27/05/2021)" enlace="https://www.upm.es/gsfs/SFS57190" textoenlace="Doctoral thesis co-supervision request procedure (UPM, in Spanish)" >}}
**It is only justified in these cases**, and the request must state the reasons:

1. **Interdisciplinary or intersectoral thesis**, where each supervisor contributes from a different field. The contribution of each must be justified.
2. **Cotutelle or inter-institutional thesis**, with a co-supervisor from another Spanish or foreign, public or private institution. The institution of each supervisor and the collaboration framework must be stated.
3. **Thesis aiming for the Industrial Doctorate mention.**
4. **Training a new supervisor** who has not yet supervised a thesis, together with an experienced supervisor who has supervised at least three.

**When to request it:**

- Preferably during the **first year** of the thesis and, as a general rule, **before the end of the second year**.
- Only **exceptionally in the third year**, clearly justified by joint scientific output: papers, projects or patents.
- **Never during an extension period**, ordinary or extraordinary.
- If granted, **you cannot submit the thesis until six months** after approval.

**Procedure:**

1. You submit the request form with the justification to the **Academic Committee**. If the co-supervisor does not belong to the programme, their **admission as a researcher** is requested at the same time.
2. The Academic Committee issues a **favourable or unfavourable report** within a maximum of **two months**.
3. The request is sent to the **Doctoral Area Committee**, which **decides** within a maximum of two months and may ask for further reports.
4. The Academic Committee informs you of the decision.

The Doctoral Area Committee may **revoke** co-supervision if it considers that it does not benefit the progress of the thesis.
{{< /general >}}

{{< programa >}}
In addition to the UPM requirements, any **stricter criteria** set out in the programme's verified report apply.
{{< /programa >}}

For **international cotutelle** theses, see [International mention and cotutelle](/en/tesis/mencion-internacional/#international-cotutelle-theses).
