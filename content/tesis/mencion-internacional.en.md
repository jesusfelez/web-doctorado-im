## "International Doctorate" mention

{{< plataforma p="thesis" >}}Mentions: pre-stay request (before the stay) and post-stay request (with the certificate){{< /plataforma >}}

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 15.1 (amended by RD 576/2023)" enlace="https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2011-2541" >}}
**All four conditions** must be met:

1. **Stays totalling at least 3 months outside Spain** at prestigious universities or research centres. If there are several, at least one must last **one month**. Your supervisor endorses them, the Academic Committee authorises them and the host institution validates them.
2. At least the **abstract and conclusions** written and defended in a **language commonly used for scientific communication** other than the official languages of Spain. This does not apply if the stays, reports and experts come from a Spanish-speaking country.
3. **Reports from at least two expert doctors** from **non-Spanish** institutions, other than those who hosted your stay.
4. At least **one examination committee member** from a **non-Spanish** institution, other than the person in charge of your stay.
{{< /general >}}

{{< programa >}}
The stay abroad is the programme's **training activity 4**, of at least 480 h. It is optional, but **compulsory** if you want the international mention. Plan it with your supervisor from the **second year**.
{{< /programa >}}

## International cotutelle theses

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 15.2" enlace="https://www.upm.es/gsfs/SFS50878" textoenlace="Cotutelle procedure and model agreement (UPM)" >}}
The degree carries the annotation **"Thesis under cotutelle with the University of…"** if:

- the thesis is supervised by doctors from **two or more universities**, one Spanish and the others foreign, under a **cotutelle agreement**;
- you obtain a degree from each institution;
- you complete a **minimum stay of 6 months** at each institution in the agreement, in one or several periods.

A cotutelle thesis may also obtain the international mention if you carry out stays at institutions other than those in the agreement.
{{< /general >}}
