## The path to the defence

1. **Pre-assessment** (from 12 months after enrolment): the Academic Committee checks the [quality requirements](/en/tesis/requisitos/) and you propose two external experts.
2. **External reports:** two doctors from outside UPM report on the thesis. You incorporate their comments and reply in writing.
3. **Submission:** you pay the examination fee, register the thesis in TESEO and submit the final version with the proposed examination committee.
4. **Public display** for 15 days at the International Doctoral School.
5. **Appointment of the examination committee** by the Doctoral Area Committee. You have a maximum of **3 months** to defend.
6. **Public defence** and grading.

Details in [Quality requirements](/en/tesis/requisitos/) and [Submission and defence](/en/tesis/deposito-defensa/).
