## Before requesting submission

{{< plataforma p="thesis" >}}Thesis submission, authorisation of the defence and proposed examination committee{{< /plataforma >}}

{{< general fuente="UPM" enlace="https://www.upm.es/Estudiantes/Estudios_Titulaciones/Estudios_Doctorado/Tesis" textoenlace="Thesis procedures and forms (UPM)" >}}
1. **Pay the thesis examination fee.**
2. **Start registering** the thesis in [TESEO](https://aplicaciones.ciencia.gob.es/teseo/).
3. Request submission **before the maximum period expires**, with your supervisor's approval.
{{< /general >}}

## Documents for submission

{{< programa titulo="What the programme's Academic Committee checks" >}}
- [ ] Thesis in **electronic format** following UPM guidelines.
- [ ] **Abstract in Spanish and in English**, up to 4,000 characters including spaces each.
- [ ] **Two favourable reports** from expert doctors outside UPM.
- [ ] **Final version** incorporating the reviewers' comments and a **reply report** justifying the changes.
- [ ] **Proposed examination committee.**
{{< /programa >}}

## Public display and examination committee

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 14 · UPM" >}}
- The EID validates the submission and opens a **15-day public display** period during which any doctor may send comments.
- If there are confidentiality agreements or patents, **two versions** are submitted: a complete confidential one and a public one, which is the one displayed.
- The **Doctoral Area Committee** approves the examination committee proposed by the Academic Committee. From then on you have **3 months** to defend. After that, a new committee must be proposed.
- **Committee membership:** all members are doctors with proven research experience, **mostly external** to the programme and to UPM, with a gender-balanced composition. Your supervisor and tutor **cannot be members**, except in cotutelles that provide for it.
- Committee members receive the thesis and the activity record **at least 7 days before** the defence.
{{< /general >}}

## Defence and grading

{{< general fuente="RD 99/2011, art. 14 · UPM" >}}
- **Public session:** you present and defend the thesis, and the doctors present may ask questions.
- **In person** at UPM, with the possible remote connection of one committee member who is neither chair nor secretary. It can also be held **by videoconference** if the Academic Committee authorises it and the whole committee agrees.
- **Grades:** fail (no apto), pass (aprobado), good (notable) or outstanding (sobresaliente). The **cum laude** distinction may be added to an outstanding grade if the committee votes for it **unanimously** by secret ballot, counted in a separate session.
- Approved theses are published in open access in the [UPM Digital Archive](https://oa.upm.es/). Confidential theses are published when the confidentiality period ends.
- **Extraordinary Doctorate Award:** one may be proposed for every ten theses defended or fraction thereof.
