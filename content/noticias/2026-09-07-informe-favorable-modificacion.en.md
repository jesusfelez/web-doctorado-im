Fundación para el Conocimiento Madri+d has issued a **final favourable report** on the modification of the PhD Programme in Mechanical Engineering, which adapts the programme report to **Royal Decree 576/2023**.

Main changes:

- **Duration:** up to 4 years full time and 7 years part time, with a possible one-year extension.
- Broader and clearer **entry profiles**, with **tailored bridging courses** of 6 to 9 ECTS from the Master's in Mechanical Engineering.
- **New training activities:** open science and dissemination of results (compulsory), international stay and line-specific training (optional).
- **Personal training plan** alongside the research plan.
- Detailed procedure for the **pre-assessment, submission and defence** of the thesis, as a monograph or as a compendium of publications.

See the details in [Admission](/en/admision/), [Training activities](/en/doctorandos/formacion/) and [Thesis requirements](/en/tesis/requisitos/).
