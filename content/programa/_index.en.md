## The programme at a glance

The PhD Programme in Mechanical Engineering trains researchers who can generate original knowledge in machines, manufacturing, vehicles and transport, acoustics, structural dynamics and laser technologies. It is run mainly by the **Department of Mechanical Engineering** of the School of Industrial Engineering (ETSII), with the participation of the **Department of Applied Physics and Materials Engineering**, UPM institutes and centres (INSIA and the Laser Centre) and researchers from other institutions.

## What you will achieve

On completing the doctorate you will be able to conceive, design and carry out an original research project, critically analyse complex ideas, communicate your results to the international scientific community and to society, and **promote open science and citizen science**.

## Outcomes

- **100% employment** of our doctors, in universities, research centres and companies.
- Since 2022, **40%** of the theses defended (22 of 56) have obtained the **international mention**.
- **20 theses defended** in 2023-24, 18 with *cum laude*.
- Mechanical Engineering at UPM regularly ranks among the world's best in the *QS World University Rankings by Subject*.
