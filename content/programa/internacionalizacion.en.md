## Research stays

{{< programa >}}
- **Training activity 4 (optional):** research stay at an institution abroad of at least **480 hours (about 3 months)**.
- We recommend doing it from the **second year** onwards and for **three consecutive months**, although it may be split into several periods where the regulations allow.
- You must provide a certificate from the host institution and a report on the stay, and include them in your annual research plan.
{{< /programa >}}

More information in [Mobility](/en/doctorandos/movilidad/) and [International mention and cotutelle](/en/tesis/mencion-internacional/).
