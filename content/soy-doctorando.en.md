<ol class="ruta">
<li><span class="r-cuando">Before applying</span>
<h3>Find a supervisor and get their commitment letter</h3>
<p>Choose a <a href="/en/programa/lineas/">research line</a> and contact a <a href="/en/programa/investigadores/">programme researcher</a>. Ask them to sign the supervision and tutoring commitment letter: <a href="/documentos/Carta_compromiso_director_tutor_misma_persona_IM.docx">template if supervisor and tutor are the same person</a> or <a href="/documentos/Carta_compromiso_director_tutor_distintos_IM.docx">if they are different people</a>. <strong>It is compulsory and must be attached to your application.</strong></p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/admision/requisitos/">Requirements</a> · <a href="/en/admision/criterios/">Criteria</a> · <a href="/en/admision/">How to apply</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">Once admitted</span>
<h3>Enrol and look for funding</h3>
<p>Enrolment is done in Automatrícula (UXXI-AC) and renewed every year. If you are full time, check the calls for predoctoral contracts.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/admision/matricula/">Enrolment</a> · <a href="/en/doctorandos/ayudas/">Grants and contracts</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">First 6 months</span>
<h3>Sign the supervision agreement</h3>
<p>Signed by you, your supervisor, your tutor and UPM. It sets out your rights and duties.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/doctorandos/">PhD candidates</a> · <a href="/en/tesis/direccion/">Supervision and tutoring</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">Before your first year is up</span>
<h3>Submit your initial research plan</h3>
<p>Use the <a href="/documentos/Plan_investigacion_inicial_IM.docx">initial plan template</a>. It includes your personal training plan. Submitted in THESIS.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/doctorandos/seguimiento/">Monitoring</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">From day one</span>
<h3>Do the training activities and log them</h3>
<p>Three compulsory activities of 1 ECTS and two optional ones, with free recommended courses. Keep the <a href="/documentos/Registro_actividades_doctorando_IM.xlsx">Excel activity log</a>: it adds up your hours and shows what is pending.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/doctorandos/formacion/">Training activities</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">Every year</span>
<h3>Annual plan and assessment</h3>
<p>Submit the annual plan and activity log on time (usually June–July). Failing to submit means withdrawal from the programme, as do two consecutive negative assessments.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/doctorandos/seguimiento/">Monitoring and annual assessment</a> · <a href="/en/doctorandos/permanencia/">Duration and extensions</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">During the thesis</span>
<h3>International stay and publications</h3>
<p>A 3-month stay abroad makes you eligible for the international mention. To defend you need at least 1 point in JCR publications.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/doctorandos/movilidad/">Mobility</a> · <a href="/en/tesis/mencion-internacional/">International mention</a> · <a href="/en/tesis/requisitos/">Quality requirements</a></p></li>

<li><span class="r-cuando">At the end</span>
<h3>Pre-assessment, deposit and defence</h3>
<p>Two favourable external reports, deposit, committee and defence. All in THESIS.</p>
<p class="r-enl"><a href="/en/tesis/deposito-defensa/">Deposit and defence</a> · <a href="/en/tesis/defendidas/">Defended theses</a></p></li>
</ol>
